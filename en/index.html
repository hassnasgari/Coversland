Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding technical specifications, wholesale ordering, and shipping

What is the difference between milky and crystal clear syringe sleeves?
Milky sleeves offer enhanced tactile friction and an anti-sweat formulation preventing slippage during gloved use, whereas crystal clear sleeves provide 100% optical visibility of buttons and syringe tips. Both versions are crafted from 100% virgin medical-grade polyethylene with continuous ultrasonic seams.
What is the minimum wholesale case quantity?
Minimum order for clinics is 1 master carton (containing 50 or 250 packs depending on item type). Special tiered discounts are provided for distributors and large network orders.
Do you offer custom OEM dimensions or private branding?
Yes, CoversLand operates specialized extrusion and converting lines capable of custom gauges, dimensions, and OEM private label packaging for wholesale orders.
