Guide to High-Efficiency Disposable Dental Suction Tubing Sleeves

Dental high-volume evacuation (HVE) and saliva ejector tubing collect significant bio-burden during oral surgery and prophylaxis. Applying perforated roll suction sleeves ensures complete containment of hazardous fluids while safeguarding clinical staff and patients.

Clinical Advantages of Continuous Suction Sleeving

In implantology and periodontal surgery, continuous handling of suction hoses can contaminate sterile gowns and dental assistant gloves. CoversLand barrier rolls provide uninterrupted coverage with precision laser perforations for instantaneous tear-off.
